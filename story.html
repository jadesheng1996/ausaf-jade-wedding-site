The Chronicle

How Two Clans Became One

Ausaf and Jade each walked their own road, gathering their own quests and companions — until those roads crossed, and one fellowship of four was forged. Here is their chronicle.

Chapter 1 of 4

Clan Farooqi

The Adventurer, Ausaf, accompanied by his two closest companions, his son Ismael and his daughter, had built for themselves a home in their tiny corner of the world. Yet, within that home, and within their party itself, something remained missing…

Chapter 2 of 4

Clan Sheng

The Artist, Jade, had wandered across the globe, finding friends, passions, hobbies, and honing her skills and talents. Little did she know her path would lead her to a brown home nestled in a forest…

Chapter 3 of 4

The Journey Begins

Together, Ausaf, Jade, Ismael, and Asiya encountered underwater behemoths in the City of Atlanta, witnessed the Milky Way in the desert sky, piloted their starship in a Galaxy Far Away, feasted among lords and knights, and laid waste to their opponents in the Fields of Fortnite. Each journey turned the four of them into something more than companions. They became family.

Chapter 4 of 4

Now We Celebrate

And thus, Ausaf and Jade, accompanied by Ismael and Asiya, shall stand before all to pledge the oath joining Clan Farooqi and Clan Sheng. Together, we shall all witness the vows, feast among friends and family, and revel until the Sun has set and the Crescent Moon shines above us all.
