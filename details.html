The Rites

The Wedding Decrees

come as the hero of your own tale

Attire for the Occasion*

This Union takes place not only in our physical world but also in the world of our hearts, imaginations and wonders. Come dressed as the hero of your own tale. Medieval, Interstellar, Fantasy. Warriors and Shield Maidens. Galactic Outlaws and Fairies.

A Solemn Decree

This is a DRY Wedding – no ale, wine, or mead shall be served. Bring only your thirst for food, joy, dessert, and fellowship.

Tributes & Blessings

There is no greater treasure or gift than your attendance. We know many of you must travel far and equip yourselves in an attire fitting for this event. We ask nothing further from you.

To those who wish to give gifts nonetheless, please note that the shelves of our home run full. A contribution of gold toward our new life is most graciously accepted.

*We are attempting to set a special ambiance for this event. We do ask that you join us in wearing a fantasy or science fiction costume of your choice. If you do not feel comfortable doing so, we ask that you dress in traditional and ethnic garb which will conform to our ambiance. Please no suits, ties, tuxedos, gowns, and casual attire. If you have questions about the attire, please reach out to us and we will be happy to assist.
